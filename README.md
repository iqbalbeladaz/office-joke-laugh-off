# Grounded for Asking for a Raise

Entry for [One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73) — one original line about office life, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The joke

> Our boss says we're a family here, so I asked for a raise and got grounded.

75 characters. The setup is the line every office worker has heard — "we're a family here" — and the turn is a single-word ambush at the very end: "grounded". The boss's family metaphor is taken literally and turned back on him, so the raise request ends like a kid being punished. Punchline lands last, the joke is clear on one read, and every word earns its place.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke, deterministically, to `/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
